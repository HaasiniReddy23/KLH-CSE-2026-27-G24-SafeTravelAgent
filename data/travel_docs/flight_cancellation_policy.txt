SkyIndia Air - Flight Cancellation and Refund Rules

If you cancel a flight more than 24 hours before departure, a cancellation fee of Rs. 500 per passenger is charged on domestic flights and the rest of the fare is refunded. If you cancel within 24 hours of departure, a cancellation fee of Rs. 1,500 per passenger is charged. If you miss the flight without cancelling, no refund is given except for airport taxes.

If the airline cancels the flight, you may choose a full refund or a free rebooking on the next available flight. Refunds are sent to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.

Baggage: domestic economy fares include 15 kg of checked baggage and 7 kg of cabin baggage.
