Goa Destination Guide

Goa is a coastal state on the west coast of India. North Goa is known for lively beaches such as Baga and Calangute, nightlife and water sports. South Goa is quieter, with beaches such as Palolem and Colva, and is popular with families and people who want rest.

The best time to visit is from November to February, when the weather is dry and pleasant. The monsoon season, from June to September, brings heavy rain, and many beach shacks close. Hotels often cost less in the monsoon season.

Getting around: renting a scooter is common, and taxis and app cabs are available in most towns. Always carry a valid driving licence if you rent a vehicle.
